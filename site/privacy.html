This policy explains what personal data the Valeria+ mobile application (“the App”) processes, for what purposes, on what legal basis, who it is shared with, and how you can exercise your rights. It follows Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD), and the Google Play Developer Program Policies, including the User Data, Sensitive Permissions and Families policies.

One-line summary: Valeria+ does not sell data, shows no advertising, embeds no analytics or ad-tracking SDKs, and builds no profiles of children. Most data never leaves the device; what does leave only does so because an authenticated professional chose to store it in their own account.

Allocation of responsibility in clinical use. When a speech therapist, clinic or association uses Valeria+ to treat patients, that professional or entity is the data controller for the clinical records they enter, and the Valeria+ project acts as a data processor for the technical hosting. The professional decides what data is entered, how long it is retained and to whom it is disclosed, and is responsible for obtaining informed consent from families.

2. Who the App is for

Valeria+ supports auditory-verbal therapy and language stimulation. It is designed to be used by a child always accompanied and supervised by an adult — a parent, legal guardian or health professional — and to be configured and administered solely by that adult.

  • Administration features (patient records, results, exports, settings) sit behind a professional access PIN.
  • The App contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no user-generated content, and no social or messaging features.
  • The App builds no advertising or behavioural profiles of children and uses no advertising identifiers (AAID) or cross-app tracking.

3. Data the App processes

Valeria+ separates three categories of data with very different journeys: data that stays on the device, data that a professional chooses to sync to the cloud, and data handled by the operating system.

Cloud sync is optional and only activates if a professional creates an account and signs in. Without an account, the App runs entirely offline.

3.3 Permissions and device data

PermissionWhy it is requestedIf you deny it
Microphone
RECORD_AUDIO		 Voice games (minimal pairs, semantic expansion, Ling test) need to hear the word the child says in order to assess whether it was produced correctly. The microphone is active only during an exercise's speaking turn, with a visible on-screen indicator, and by default does not open automatically: a deliberate action by the adult or child starts listening. Voice exercises are disabled. The rest of the App keeps working.
Camera
CAMERA		 The Augmented Reality exercises use the front camera as a motion sensor: they measure whether the child rounds their lips, turns their head towards a sound or holds their gaze on a picture, and those gestures are what make the 3D figures react. The camera is active only inside one of those exercises, with the image visible on screen and the system indicator showing, and it switches off on exit. The App does not record, store or upload any image or video: each frame is analysed on the device itself and discarded immediately; only numeric measurements are kept (degrees of turn, milliseconds, ratios). No biometric identification takes place: facial points are used to measure motor behaviour, never to recognise or identify a person. The Augmented Reality block does not appear. The other six therapy blocks work exactly the same.

About the camera in the Augmented Reality exercises. These three statements are not declarations of intent: they are design constraints of the code, verifiable in the project's public repository.

  • No video frame leaves the device. Image analysis runs entirely on the phone, with no network connection and no third-party components sending data of their own.
  • No video frame is stored. There is no recording, no screenshot and no temporary copy on disk: the image is processed and released in the same instant. Only derived numeric values persist.
  • There is no biometric identification. The App does not compare faces, does not build facial templates and cannot recognise anyone. It measures gestures, not identities. This processing therefore does not constitute processing of biometric data for the purpose of uniquely identifying a natural person within the meaning of Article 9(1) GDPR.

Were a video-recording feature ever added, this statement would cease to be true and the policy would be updated before that version was published.

About voice recordings. Valeria+ does not store, replay or upload any audio file of the child to its servers. Audio is processed ephemerally during the speaking turn and discarded; the App keeps only the outcome of the assessment (hit or miss).

Where that audio is recognised. The App asks the system to perform speech recognition on the device itself, so that the child's audio does not leave the phone. This is not stated as a universal guarantee, because it is not one: it depends on two conditions the App does not control — that the device is capable of offline recognition, and that the relevant language pack has been downloaded. The decision is made separately for each speech variety: the pack is commonly available for Castilian Spanish, and considerably less likely for Galician and Basque.

Where on-device recognition is unavailable, the App continues to work using the operating system's speech recognition service — typically Google's — and that service may process the audio on its own servers under its own privacy policy, which is outside Valeria+'s control. This is a deliberate choice: interrupting a rehabilitation exercise on these grounds would harm the child more than the problem it seeks to avoid.

The App always shows which of the two modes is in use for the active variety, in the “Voz de la app” card, and offers to download the offline language pack where the device supports it.

5. Who data is shared with

Valeria+ does not sell or transfer personal data to third parties for commercial or advertising purposes. The only disclosures are:

  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC), as the provider of Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, hosts the professional accounts and patient records described in section 3.2, acting as a data processor. See the Firebase privacy and security terms.
  • The device's speech recognition service (usually Google), only where recognition cannot be performed on the device itself, as described in section 3.3.
  • Recipients chosen by the adult when they export a report or log and share it through Android's share sheet (for example, by email). That disclosure is initiated and fully controlled by the person performing it.
  • Public authorities, where there is a legal obligation to disclose.

6. International transfers

Firebase infrastructure may process data on servers located outside the European Economic Area. Google covers those transfers with the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. No other international transfers take place.

7. Retention periods

  • Local data (preferences, progress, PIN): for as long as the App is installed. Erased on uninstall or on clearing app data.
  • Usability telemetry: purged automatically after a successful export and, in any case, bounded by weekly windows and a maximum number of events per session.
  • Professional account and synced records: until the professional deletes them or requests account deletion. Clinical retention periods are determined by the professional or the clinic under applicable healthcare law.

8. Security

  • All communication with Firebase is encrypted in transit with TLS.
  • Cloud Firestore rules deny by default and isolate each professional's data.
  • Telemetry is encrypted at rest in the App's private storage.
  • The professional PIN is stored as a cryptographic digest, never in clear text.
  • Production builds apply code obfuscation and shrinking.
  • The source code is publicly auditable at github.com/FrankBetances/Valeria.

No system is infallible: if you find a vulnerability, please report it following the procedure in SECURITY.md.

9. Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and withdraw consent at any time, by writing to frank.alberto.betances.reinoso@gmail.com. We respond within one month at the latest.

If the data concerns a patient record created by a speech therapist or a clinic, please contact that professional or entity first, as they decide over that data.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency or your country's supervisory authority.

Instructions to delete your account and data are on the data deletion page.

10. Children

Valeria+ processes children's data only when a responsible adult — a parent, legal guardian or authorised health professional — enters it into the App. The App does not request data directly from the child and does not let them register, publish content or communicate with third parties. There is no advertising directed at children and no profiling with legal or similarly significant effects.
